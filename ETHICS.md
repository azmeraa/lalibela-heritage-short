**# ETHICS.md**



**## Project: Lalibela — A Story Carved in Stone**



**## 1. Purpose and Audience**



**This film introduces international audiences to Lalibela, Ethiopia, and its remarkable rock-hewn churches. It aims to encourage appreciation of Ethiopian history, religious heritage, cultural diversity, and shared human heritage.**



**The intended audience includes international travelers, heritage enthusiasts, and people interested in Ethiopian culture.**



**## 2. AI-Generated Content Disclosure**



**AI tools were used to generate visual images, narration, and original music.**



**The film combines AI-generated media with human creative direction, storyboard development, editing, and review.**



**The end card includes an AI disclosure so viewers understand that AI-generated content was used in production.**



**## 3. Representation of People**



**The international visitors and Ethiopian guide shown in the film are fictional AI-generated characters.**



**No real person's likeness was intentionally used, and no real person was recorded or impersonated for the film.**



**The narration was generated using text-to-speech technology.**



**## 4. Cultural and Religious Respect**



**Lalibela is an important religious and cultural heritage site.**



**The production aims to represent the rock-hewn churches, Ethiopian Orthodox Christian traditions, and pilgrims respectfully.**



**Visual prompts were written to avoid invented religious symbols, disrespectful depictions, and misleading architectural details.**



**AI-generated images can contain inaccuracies. The images should therefore be understood as illustrative representations, not historical photographic evidence.**



**## 5. Historical and Cultural Accuracy**



**The film focuses on the distinctive rock-hewn architecture of Lalibela, including Biete Ghiorgis (the Church of Saint George).**



**Prompts were designed to encourage historically respectful and geographically appropriate representations.**



**However, AI-generated visuals may contain errors. Future versions should include review by local heritage experts or knowledgeable community members.**



**## 6. Privacy and Consent**



**No real individuals were intentionally depicted or recorded for this film.**



**All people shown are fictional AI-generated characters, and the narration uses synthetic speech.**



**The project does not claim that fictional characters are actual visitors, pilgrims, or local residents.**



**## 7. Potential Bias and Limitations**



**AI image-generation systems may reproduce stereotypes, inaccurate cultural details, or unrealistic human appearances.**



**The production attempted to reduce these risks through descriptive prompts, review, and selection of suitable images.**



**The film is a short creative introduction and should not be treated as a comprehensive historical or religious account of Lalibela.**



**## 8. Transparency and Responsibility**



**The creator is responsible for reviewing generated content before publication.**



**The final film should be shared with an AI-generated-content disclosure where the publishing platform provides an appropriate label.**



**The project aims to use AI as a creative and educational tool while respecting Ethiopian cultural heritage and the dignity of the communities represented.**



**## 9. Future Improvements**



**Future versions could:**



**- Consult local historians, heritage experts, and community members.**

**- Improve historical and architectural verification.**

**- Gather feedback from Ethiopian audiences and international viewers.**

**- Make the film accessible through accurate subtitles and clear narration.**

**- Maintain transparent documentation of AI-generated assets and production decisions.**



**## 10. Conclusion**



**AI can help make cultural storytelling more accessible, but it must be used thoughtfully.**



**This project seeks to celebrate Lalibela's heritage while acknowledging the limitations of generated media and the importance of cultural accuracy, transparency, and respect.**
